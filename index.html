📚 Safety Guides

Remember these essential steps during common emergencies.

🌎

Earthquake Safety

Drop, Cover and Hold On.
Protect your head and neck under sturdy furniture if possible.
Stay away from windows and heavy objects.
Do not use elevators. After shaking stops, evacuate carefully if needed.
🔥

Fire Safety

Raise the alarm and get out using a safe exit.
Stay low under smoke and never use an elevator.
Never go back inside a burning building.
Call the local fire service from a safe place.
🌧️

Flood & Heavy Rain

Move to higher ground when flooding threatens.
Never walk or drive through floodwater.
Stay away from drains, rivers and electrical wires.
Follow official evacuation instructions.
⚡

Electrical Safety

Never touch exposed or fallen electrical wires.
Keep away from electrical equipment in wet areas.
Do not touch someone receiving an electric shock until power is safely disconnected.
Call trained emergency responders for assistance.
🩹

First Aid Basics

Check that the scene is safe before approaching.
Call emergency services for serious injuries.
For severe bleeding, apply firm direct pressure with a clean cloth if safe.
Do not move someone with a suspected serious neck or back injury unless there is immediate danger.
🏫

School Safety

Know your exits and school assembly point.
Follow teachers and emergency wardens.
Keep corridors and exits clear.
Never spread unverified emergency rumours.
