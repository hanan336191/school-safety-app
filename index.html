🛡️ Safety Guides

Learn what to do before, during and after common emergencies.

🌎

Earthquake Safety

Know what to do when the ground starts shaking.

  • Drop, Cover and Hold On.
  • Protect your head and neck.
  • Stay away from windows and heavy objects.
  • Do not use elevators.
  • After shaking stops, leave damaged buildings carefully.
🔥

Fire Safety

Stay calm and follow your school's evacuation plan.

  • Raise the alarm and leave immediately.
  • Use stairs, not elevators.
  • Stay low if smoke is present.
  • Never re-enter a burning building.
  • Do not hide from firefighters.
🌧️

Heavy Rain & Floods

Floodwater can be deeper and more dangerous than it looks.

  • Move to higher ground when instructed.
  • Never walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Stay away from drains and flowing water.
  • Keep away from fallen electrical wires.
  • Follow official weather warnings.
⚡

Lightning & Storms

Find safe shelter when thunder and lightning begin.

  • Go inside a substantial building or enclosed vehicle.
  • Avoid open fields and isolated trees.
  • Stay away from water and metal fences.
  • Unplugging devices is only safe before a storm.
  • Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder before going outside.
🔌

Electrical Short Circuit

Electrical hazards require distance and trained help.

  • Do not touch exposed wires or sparks.
  • Never touch an electric shock victim while power may be on.
  • Warn others and move away.
  • Tell a teacher or responsible adult immediately.
  • Leave if smoke or fire develops.
🏫

Unsafe School Building

Report damage before someone gets hurt.

  • Stay away from cracked or collapsing areas.
  • Report damaged ceilings, walls and stairs.
  • Do not enter restricted areas.
  • Follow evacuation instructions.
  • Wait for authorized staff to declare an area safe.
🚪

Intruder & Security Threat

Follow your school's official emergency instructions.

  • Alert a teacher or security staff if safe.
  • Move away from danger if there is a safe route.
  • If escape is unsafe, follow the school's lockdown plan.
  • Silence your phone when instructed.
  • Call police when safe to do so.
🤝

Fighting & Bullying

Protect yourself without making the situation worse.

  • Move away from the fight.
  • Do not encourage or join in.
  • Tell a trusted teacher or adult.
  • Do not put yourself in danger by intervening physically.
  • Report threats and repeated bullying.
🩹

First Aid & Injuries

Get appropriate help and avoid unnecessary movement.

  • Tell a teacher or responsible adult.
  • Call emergency services for serious injuries.
  • Do not move someone with a suspected neck or spine injury unless danger requires it.
  • Do not give food or drink to an unconscious person.
  • Follow instructions from emergency dispatchers.
