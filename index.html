Explore Safety Categories

Simple steps to help you stay safe during emergencies.

🌧️

Rain & Flood Safety

Stay safe during heavy rain, flooding and thunderstorms.

  • Move to higher ground if flooding threatens.
  • Never walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Stay away from fallen electrical wires.
  • Follow official weather and emergency alerts.
🌎

Earthquake Safety

Know what to do during and after an earthquake.

  • Drop, Cover and Hold On.
  • Protect your head and neck.
  • Stay away from windows and heavy objects.
  • When shaking stops, evacuate if instructed.
🔥

Fire Safety

Learn how to respond safely to a fire.

  • Raise the alarm and leave immediately.
  • Use stairs, never elevators.
  • Stay low if there is smoke.
  • Never re-enter a burning building.
🚑

Medical Emergency

Respond carefully when someone is injured or unwell.

  • Call a responsible adult and emergency services.
  • Do not move someone with a possible serious injury.
  • Do not give an unconscious person food or drink.
  • Follow instructions from emergency professionals.
⚡

Electrical Safety

Avoid electrical hazards at school and at home.

  • Never touch exposed wires.
  • Keep electrical devices away from water.
  • Stay far away from fallen power lines.
  • Tell an adult about damaged equipment.
🛡️

Personal Safety

Make safer choices and know when to ask for help.

  • Stay with trusted adults in emergencies.
  • Tell a teacher about unsafe situations.
  • Keep emergency contacts accessible.
  • Follow your school's evacuation plan.
